About

A career built at the intersection of technology and text

Since 2011, I have led the technical infrastructure and computer-assisted education programs at Özel Getronagan High School in Istanbul — from managing IT procurement and network systems to training teachers, instructing Python for the Computer Science curriculum, and mentoring students through the school's Robotics and Computer Clubs.

Earlier in my career, I spent several years in the Netherlands working as a Project Consultant and Functional Application Engineer for organizations including Mansystems, VCS Energy and ICT Group, and New York Pizza BV — translating client requirements into technical specifications and managing software releases for international teams.

In parallel, I've spent over a decade in publishing as a copy editor, proofreader, and layout coordinator for İnkılap Publishing House, İstanbul Kültür A.Ş., and Tefrika Publishing — carrying manuscripts from dictation through to final page design.
